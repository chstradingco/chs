About the company

Pure, natural, healthy — from nature to you.

Choudhary Harisingh Trading Company (CHS) is a Rajasthan-based proprietorship, trading in natural food products since 2005. We pack pure honey under our own CHS — Honey & Natural Products label and export it as an APEDA-registered merchant exporter.

Our honey is sourced from trusted beekeepers and natural floral sources across India — pure, unadulterated and packed with care. Every shipment carries full export documentation, so buyers can trade with complete confidence.
